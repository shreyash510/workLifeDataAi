1. A Small Story First

First week on the project. In one morning stand-up you hear:

"The Tariff on Coworker 4471 changed, so the resident MRR in fct_contract_month dropped, but NetSuite recognised the full amount because it was deferred. Can you check if Reading's period is closed before the board pack?"

That's one sentence with eight domain terms from four different worlds: Nexudus (Tariff, Coworker), the business (resident, MRR), the warehouse (fct_contract_month), and finance (recognised, deferred, period closed, board pack). If any one of those words is fuzzy, you'll nod, go back to your desk and query the wrong thing. 😅

Interview Questions

Q: Someone says "Soho's revenue was $4,456.25 in September". What do you ask?

"Is that invoiced from Nexudus or recognised in NetSuite, and is September closed? In our lab, invoiced is $4,456.25 and recognised is $4,343.75; the $112.50 gap is a credit memo that exists only in NetSuite. For the board pack, recognised revenue from a closed period is the number."

Q: What's the difference between a Coworker and a Customer in Nexudus?

"None. Coworker is the old API and database name; Customer is what the screens show. Same with Business for Location and Tariff for Plan. You rename them once in dbt staging."

Q: Explain deferred revenue to a non-accountant.

"If a member prepays a year, we haven't earned it yet. Finance records it as a liability and moves one month into revenue each month as we deliver the space. So the invoice is big in January, but recognised revenue is the same every month."

Q: Why is "source of truth" decided per field rather than per system?

"Because each system is best at different facts. HubSpot owns names, emails and sales data; Nexudus owns contracts, bookings and invoices; NetSuite owns recognised revenue and costs. Saying 'HubSpot is the truth' for everything would break billing."

Key Points to Remember

ConceptKey Takeaway
Four worldsBusiness, finance, systems, data/AI. Know which world a word comes from.
Old namesBusiness, Coworker, Tariff = Location, Customer, Plan.
RevenueInvoiced ≠ recognised ≠ cash. Board pack = recognised, closed period.
ChurnOnly a company leaving with no follow-on contract.
Trap wordsRevenue, occupancy, churn, member, customer, site, profitable, ACT, month, active.
When unsureAsk "which one do you mean?" It's the most valuable question on this project.
