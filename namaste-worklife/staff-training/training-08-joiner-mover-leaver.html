Staff Training 08 — Joiner, Mover, Leaver: What to Do in Every System

Hi again! 🙏 This is the most important guide in the training, and it is for everyone: front desk, site managers, sales, billing and finance. Every member does three things in their life with us: they join, they sometimes move (bigger, smaller, another site, a new term), and one day they leave. Each of these touches four or five systems, and each step must happen in the right order, by the right person.

Get these flows right and the systems agree, members get in the door on day one, invoices are right, and the business reports tell the truth about who joined, who grew and who left.

⚠️ Before you start. This is our standard way of working (🛠). Screen names change, so check the vendor's help centre (📘) when a button has moved. Example members and prices come from Mini Work.Life, a made-up company (lab files contracts.csv, members.csv, act_opportunities.csv). Notice periods, deposits and how part-months are charged depend on your own contracts and setup (❓ ask your manager).

What we will cover in this guide:

  • The one rule behind all of this
  • Joiner: a new member company, and one more person in an existing company
  • Movers: upgrade, downgrade, transfer between sites, renewal
  • Leaver: a whole company leaving, and one person leaving a company that stays
  • Why it matters: the "wrong way" example
  • Printable checklists for each flow
  • Practice tasks and a quick check

1. The One Rule Behind All of This

🛠 The company (the Nexudus "team") is the member. A contract is just what they have right now. When what they have changes, we end the old contract and start a new one on the same team. We never cancel the member and create them again as a stranger.

The reports then read each contract end like this (from Chapter 07):

   A contract ENDS. Does the SAME team have another contract
   starting soon after (within about 30 days; the business sets the exact gap)?

     YES, same site, higher price  →  UPGRADE    (a member growing 🎉)
     YES, same site, lower price   →  DOWNGRADE  (smaller, but still here)
     YES, different site           →  TRANSFER   (moved, not lost)
     YES, same office, new term    →  RENEWAL    (stayed)
     NO                            →  CHURN      (they left: record the reason)

This only works if you keep the same team, put real dates in, and record notice and reasons. That is what every flow below does.

2. Joiner: A New Member Company

Lab example: GreenRoot Foods. The deal "GreenRoot - Office 201" was won on 10 Dec 2025 at $3,375 a month. The contract (K005) runs 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2026 for Office 201 at Reading, and 6 people joined on 1 Jan.

#StepSystemWho
1Mark the deal Closed Won, with the won price, start date and site. Check the company and main contact are linked to the deal.HubSpotSales
2Send the signed agreement and details (people, start date, price, office) to the site team. ❓ Your company may use a form or a HubSpot task for this.HubSpot / emailSales
3Search first, then create the team (the company) with the billing contact and billing emailNexudusFront desk / site manager
4Create a customer for each person and add them to the team. Same email as in HubSpot.NexudusFront desk
5Create the contract: plan, office, site, start date, end date (or none if rolling), price. The price must match the won price in HubSpot.NexudusFront desk / site manager
6Take the deposit if your company asks for one (❓ how it is recorded)NexudusBilling
7Give door access to each person (automatic if Nexudus is connected; otherwise by hand). Test on day one.ACT365Site manager (Guide 06)
8Check the first invoice: right price, right site, part-month charged the way your setup says (❓)Nexudus → NetSuiteBilling (Guide 03)
9Welcome: tour, Wi-Fi, app login, house rulesNexudus app, in personCommunity / front desk
10Check the office is in the capacity list (only if it is new)ExcelOperations (Guide 07)

One more person in an existing company. Made-up example: Nomad Analytics (Ravi's company, hot desk at Soho) takes on a colleague who also wants a hot desk.

#StepSystemWho
1Search for the person. Create the customer and add them to the existing team. Never create a second "Nomad Analytics".NexudusFront desk
2If they need their own plan, add a contract on the same team (made up: Unlimited Coworking, $400). If they share an office the company already pays for, no new contract; just check the office has room.NexudusFront desk
3Add the contact to the company in HubSpot. If it is extra sales (a new plan), log a deal too (❓ your sales rules).HubSpotSales / front desk
4Give door accessACT365Site manager

💡 Check capacity before adding people to an office. Pixel Chai's Office 101 holds 4 people, and Pixel Chai already has 4. A fifth person means a bigger office (an upgrade), not a squeeze.

3. Mover: Upgrade

Lab example: Priya at Pixel Chai. Priya had Unlimited Coworking at Soho (K001, $400, from 1 Oct 2025). The upgrade deal "Pixel Chai - Office 101" was won on 14 Mar 2026: $3,250 list, won at $3,000 (a 7.7% discount for a 12-month term).

#StepSystemWho
1Log the upgrade as a new deal on the same company and mark it Closed Won at $3,000. Do not create a new company.HubSpotSales
2End the old contract K001 on 31 Mar 2026, with the end reason "upgrade" if your setup has that field (❓). Do not cancel the customer or the team.NexudusFront desk / site manager
3Start the new contract K002 on 1 Apr 2026: Private Office, Office 101, $3,000 (list $3,250), end 31 Mar 2027, on the same teamNexudusFront desk / site manager
4Add the 3 new people (Arjun, Leah, Tom, all joined 1 Apr) to the same teamNexudusFront desk
5Door access: Priya moves from "Soho Coworking" to "Soho Office 101" on 1 Apr; the 3 new people go into "Soho Office 101". Same card for Priya.ACT365Site manager
6Check the April invoice shows the office, not the hot desk. Here the change is on a month boundary, so no part-month. A mid-month change would need a part-month charge or credit (❓ how your setup does it).Nexudus → NetSuiteBilling

4. Mover: Downgrade

No downgrade in the lab. Made-up example: a company moves from a 4-person office to two hot desks at the same site.

#StepSystemWho
1Log the change on the same company (❓ as a deal or a note, by your sales rules). Record why (for example "team got smaller").HubSpotSales / account manager
2Check the notice terms of the old contract before agreeing the date (❓)Nexudus / signed agreementSite manager
3End the office contract on its last day; start the new hot desk contracts the next day, same teamNexudusFront desk
4Move people from the office group to the coworking group; remove anyone who is leavingACT365Site manager
5Check the next invoice, and any deposit change (❓)Nexudus → NetSuiteBilling
6Tell sales the office is free to sellHubSpotSite manager

5. Mover: Transfer Between Sites

Lab example: Fatima at Kite & Key. Fatima had Flex 10 Days at Reading (K006, $225, 1 Feb to 31 Jul 2026), then moved to Unlimited Coworking at Soho (K007, $400, from 1 Aug, rolling).

#StepSystemWho
1Agree the move and the date. Both site managers know.Email / in personOld and new site managers
2End K006 at Reading on 31 Jul (end reason "transfer" if you have the field)NexudusOld site
3Start K007 at Soho on 1 Aug, same team, same customer. Pick the right site: this decides where the income lands.NexudusNew site
4Remove Reading access; add "Soho Coworking". Same card.ACT365Site managers
5Update the company's site in HubSpot if you track it there (❓)HubSpotSales
6Check the August invoice is at Soho, at $400Nexudus → NetSuiteBilling

💡 For the business, nobody left. For Reading it is a loss, and for Soho a gain. The reports can show both, but only if the team stays the same.

6. Mover: Renewal

Lab example: GreenRoot. Contract K005 (Office 201, $3,375) ends 31 Dec 2026. The renewal deal "GreenRoot - Renewal 2027" was opened on 15 Sep 2026, and a proposal went out at $3,500. Stage: Proposal Sent.

#StepSystemWho
1Open a renewal deal in the Renewals pipeline, 🛠 120 days before the term ends (made up; the same rule as Guide 04), so it starts well before the notice window. GreenRoot's was opened on 15 Sep, only 107 days before 31 Dec, so later than our standard.HubSpotAccount manager / sales
2Talk to the member; send the proposal; move the deal through its stagesHubSpotAccount manager
3aIf they sign: deal Closed Won. Then in Nexudus, either start a new contract the day after the old one ends (same team, same office, new price and term) or extend the existing contract. ❓ Ask your manager which one your company uses, and always do it the same way.HubSpot, NexudusSales, then front desk
3bIf they say no: deal Closed Lost with the lost reason, and follow the Leaver flow (section 7)HubSpotSales
4Check the first invoice at the new priceNexudus → NetSuiteBilling

💡 Door access does not change on a renewal, unless the office changes. But keep an eye out: GreenRoot's door use fell from 73% to about 0 across September. If the renewal is open and the team has stopped coming in, tell the account manager this week.

7. Leaver: A Whole Company Leaves

Lab example: Brightline Legal. Office 103 at Soho (K003, $1,750, 2 people). The renewal deal opened 1 Mar 2026. Brightline gave notice on 30 Apr 2026, reason "moving to hybrid". The contract ended on 30 Jun 2026.

WhenStepSystemWho
Notice day (30 Apr)Record the notice date and the reason on the contract (❓ which fields your setup has). Set the contract end date (30 Jun). Do not delete or cancel anything.NexudusSite manager
Notice dayMark the renewal deal Closed Lost with the lost reason from the drop-down (here: hybrid/remote), plus a short noteHubSpotAccount manager
Notice dayTell sales the office will be free from 1 Jul, so they can start selling itHubSpotSite manager
Notice periodKeep billing as normal. Agree the move-out date, key and card return, and the office check.NexudusBilling, site manager
Last invoiceCheck the last invoice covers up to the end date only, and nothing is billed after it. Settle any extras (meeting rooms) and any unpaid invoices.Nexudus → NetSuiteBilling
DepositReturn the deposit, or use it against unpaid amounts, by your rules (❓). The lab has no deposits.Nexudus / NetSuiteBilling, finance
Last day (30 Jun)Contract ends on its own on the end date. Check it shows as ended, not deleted.NexudusSite manager
Last dayRemove door access for both people; collect cards. (In the lab both Brightline cards show "removed", and an old card was denied on 2 Sep.)ACT365Site manager
AfterMake sure the company is shown as a former customer, not a current one (❓ your HubSpot setup). Lifecycle stage does not move backwards by itself.HubSpotSales
AfterOffboarding: remove Wi-Fi and app access, empty the office, forward post (❓ how long)Nexudus, in personFront desk

One person leaves a company that stays. Made-up example: one of GreenRoot's 6 people leaves GreenRoot.

#StepSystemWho
1The company's main contact tells us. Note the date.Nexudus (note)Front desk
2Remove the person from the team, or mark them as left (❓ your setup). Do not touch the company's office contract.NexudusFront desk
3If the person had their own plan, end that contract (end reason "left")NexudusFront desk
4Remove door access for that person onlyACT365Site manager
5Update the contact in HubSpot (no longer at the company)HubSpotSales

8. Why It Matters: The "Wrong Way" Example

Imagine a busy front desk takes a shortcut: for Priya's upgrade and Fatima's transfer, they cancel the old member and create a brand new customer. Here is what the 2026 reports would say:

2026 reportWrong way (cancel and create new)Right way (same team)
Companies that left3 (Pixel Chai, Brightline, Kite & Key)1 (Brightline)
Monthly income lost to leavers$2,375 (K001 + K003 + K006)$1,750 (Brightline only)
New monthly income from new members$7,000 (K002 + K005 + K006 + K007)$3,600 (GreenRoot + Kite & Key's first plan)
Priya's historySplit across two "people"; looks like a leaver and a strangerOne member who grew from $400 to $3,000
Kite & KeyReading "lost a customer", Soho "won one"One member who moved site

The wrong way makes the business look like it loses members three times faster than it does, and hides its best news (a member growing). Leaders could make real decisions on fake numbers. Five extra minutes doing it the right way prevents all of it.

  2. In the training area, do Priya's upgrade end to end: end the hot desk on 31 Mar, start Office 101 on 1 Apr on the same team, and write down the door group change.

Quick check

1. A member moves from a hot desk to an office at the same site. What do you do in Nexudus?
End the old contract on its last day and start the new one the next day, on the same team. Never cancel the member and re-create them.

2. Brightline gives notice. What two things must you record?
The notice date and the reason. And do not delete anything.

3. Kite & Key moves from Reading to Soho. Is that a leaver?
No, it is a transfer. Same team, new contract at the new site, door access changed, invoice at the new site.

4. When should a renewal deal be opened?
120 days before the term ends (our made-up standard), well before the notice window. GreenRoot's was opened 15 Sep for a 31 Dec end: 107 days, so later than the standard.

5. One person leaves a company that stays. Whose door access do you remove?
That one person's only. The company's contract and other people stay as they are.
