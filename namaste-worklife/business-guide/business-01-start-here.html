Business Guide 01 — Start Here: What the Data System Does for You

Hi! 🙏 This short set of guides is for the business people who use the numbers every day: the leadership team, finance, site and operations managers, and sales. You do not need to know anything about data, SQL or the tools behind it.

The data team has connected your business systems (Nexudus, NetSuite, HubSpot, ACT and your Excel sheets) into one place, checked the numbers, and built a set of dashboards. These guides show you how to read them, how far you can trust them, and the small things you do every day that keep them right.

⚠️ About the examples. All example numbers in these guides come from Mini Work.Life, a small made-up company with two sites (Soho and Reading) used across this series. Your own dashboards will show your real numbers. Dashboard names and layouts here are our standard set; your team may name or arrange them a little differently.

1. What the Data System Is, in One Picture

Before, each team had its own numbers in its own system, and someone built the monthly reports by hand. Now every night the data is copied from each system into one place, checked, and turned into the dashboards.

   WHERE YOUR DATA IS ENTERED              WHAT YOU SEE
   ──────────────────────────              ────────────

   Nexudus   members, contracts,  ─┐
             bookings, invoices    │
   NetSuite  the accounts         ─┤      every night         ┌──► Leadership Overview
   HubSpot   leads, deals         ─┼──►  copied, cleaned, ──►─┼──► Site P&L
   ACT       door access or CRM   ─┤      and checked         ├──► Members & Retention
   Excel     budget, capacity     ─┘                          ├──► Meeting Rooms
                                                              └──► Sales Pipeline

Two ideas matter most:

  • The dashboards are only as good as what goes in. If a contract is entered with the wrong site, the dashboard shows it at the wrong site. Guide 05 shows the rules that keep this right.
  • Everyone sees the same number. "Revenue" means one thing, worked out one way, for every team. Guide 03 lists every number and exactly what it means.

4. Five Things to Know Before You Look at Any Number

#Know thisWhy it matters
1The data is from last night, not this minute.A contract signed this morning shows up tomorrow. The "last updated" time is on every dashboard.
2An open month is "provisional". Its numbers can still change until finance closes the month.Do not quote this month's profit as final. Guide 04 explains why.
3Recurring income (MRR) and revenue are different numbers.MRR is what contracts promise each month; revenue is what finance counts in the accounts. Both are right, they answer different questions (Guide 03).
4Occupancy is "desks filled ÷ desks you can sell", worked out across all sites together, not an average of each site's percentage.In Mini Work.Life, September 2026: 12 of 65 desks = 18.5% (made-up example). When sites are very different sizes, averaging their percentages gives a wrong answer; Guide 03 shows an example.
5If a number looks wrong, say so. Do not fix it in your own spreadsheet.A private fix means two versions of the truth. Guide 06 shows the 2-minute way to report it.
