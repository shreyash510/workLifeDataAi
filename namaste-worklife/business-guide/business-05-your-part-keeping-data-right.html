Business Guide 05 — Your Part: Entering Data So the Numbers Are Right

Hi again! 🙏 The dashboards do not make up numbers. They add up what people type into Nexudus, HubSpot, NetSuite, ACT and the Excel sheets. So the person who enters a contract, a deal or an invoice line is really the first person who builds the dashboard. This guide shows the small habits that keep the numbers right.

⚠️ About the examples. All example numbers come from Mini Work.Life, the small made-up company with two sites (Soho and Reading). The rules below are our standard set. Your data owner may add rules that fit your company.

What we will cover in this guide:

  • Why one wrong entry gives a wrong dashboard
  • Nexudus: contracts, bookings and members
  • Upgrades and transfers: the mistake that looks like a lost customer
  • HubSpot: companies, contacts and deals

1. Why One Wrong Entry Gives a Wrong Dashboard

Every night the data is copied from each system and added up. Nothing in between can guess what you meant. If a contract says "Reading", the dashboards count it at Reading, even if the office is in Soho.

Here is what happens in Mini Work.Life if one contract, Pixel Chai's Office 101 at £2,400 a month, is saved with the wrong site:

Recurring income (MRR), September 2026SohoReading
Entered correctly£3,040£2,700
Office 101 saved at Reading by mistake£640£5,100

One wrong drop-down, and Soho looks almost empty while Reading looks like a star. The Site P&L, the occupancy figure and the board pack would all be wrong in the same way. The total for the company is still right, which is why nobody notices at first.

💡 The big rule: fix mistakes in the system where they were entered, not in a dashboard or a spreadsheet. The next night's copy then puts every report right at once.

2. Nexudus: Contracts, Bookings and Members

Nexudus is the biggest source of numbers. It holds members, contracts, bookings and invoices, so most of the rules are here.

✅ Do❌ Don'tWhat goes wrong if you don't
Pick the right site (location) on every contract and every booking.Leave the default site because it was already selected.Income, desks and room use land at the wrong site (section 1).
Set the end date on fixed-term contracts, and record the notice date on the day the member gives notice.Keep notice in an email or your head until the last day.Renewals look safe when they are not. Members & Retention cannot warn you early.
Record the reason someone leaves, picked from the list (plus a short note if you like).Leave it blank, or type a long free-text story only."Why do members leave?" cannot be answered. Free text cannot be counted.
Do an upgrade, downgrade or move to another site as exactly that (section 3).Cancel the old contract as "left" and start a brand-new customer.A happy, growing member shows up as a lost customer.
Search first (name, email, company) before you add a customer or company.Create a second record for someone who is already there.The member is counted twice; their history is split in two.
Keep test accounts off live sites. If you must have one, name it clearly (for example "TEST - do not bill") and tell the data owner.Make a "fake" member on a real site to try something out, then forget it.Member counts and occupancy go up for no reason.
Cancel a booking that will not happen, using the cancel button.Leave it, or change it to a different room to "reuse" it.Meeting Rooms shows rooms busier than they were, and income that never came.
Put prices and discounts in the proper fields.Hide a discount in a note.Discounts cannot be seen or added up.

❓ Fill in for your company: the list of leaving reasons everyone should use, and who keeps the list of test and staff accounts.

3. Upgrades and Transfers: the Mistake That Looks Like a Lost Customer

To Nexudus, an upgrade is just "one contract ended, another started". The data system looks at what happened next to decide what kind of change it was:

   A contract ends.  Did the same company start a new one straight away?

      YES, same site, higher price   ──►  UPGRADE    (good news)
      YES, same site, lower price    ──►  DOWNGRADE
      YES, a different site          ──►  TRANSFER   (still a customer)
      NO                             ──►  CHURN      (a lost customer)

This only works if you keep it as the same company. In Mini Work.Life during 2026:

What really happenedEntered the right way, it shows asEntered as "cancel and new customer", it shows as
Pixel Chai moved from a £320 hot desk to Office 101 at £2,400 (1 April)UpgradeA lost customer (£320) plus a "new" company
Kite & Key moved from Reading (£180) to a Soho hot desk (1 August)TransferA lost customer (£180) plus a "new" company
Brightline left on 30 June, "moving to hybrid"Churn: £1,400 a monthChurn: £1,400 a month
Lost customers in 20261 company, £1,400 a month3 companies, £1,900 a month

So two quick shortcuts would make it look like the business lost three customers instead of one, and the board would ask the wrong questions. When in doubt, ask the data owner before you cancel anything.

4. HubSpot: Companies, Contacts and Deals

HubSpot is being set up to become the main place for member details over time. That makes clean records here more important every month.

✅ Do❌ Don'tWhat goes wrong if you don't
Search before you create a contact or company (try the email, the website and part of the name).Create a new one because the name looked a bit different.Duplicates. In Mini Work.Life the same company is "Pixel Chai Studio" in one system and "Pixel Chai Studio Ltd" in another, and one person's email is written two ways. Names alone are not enough.
If you find a duplicate, use HubSpot's merge, and keep the record that has the Nexudus ID as the main one.Delete one copy, or rename it "OLD".History, deals and notes are lost or split. The link to Nexudus breaks.
Fill in the Nexudus ID field on every member company and contact.Leave it blank "for later".The data system cannot be sure a HubSpot record and a Nexudus record are the same member.
Use the drop-down values (for example site, member status, reason lost).Type your own words in a free-text box."Soho", "SOHO" and "soho office" count as three different sites.
Keep each deal's stage, amount and close date up to date, every week.Leave old deals open with close dates in the past.The Sales Pipeline shows money that will never come, and the win rate is wrong.
Mark lost deals as lost, with a reason.Just stop working on them.Nobody can learn why deals are lost.

❓ Fill in for your company: the exact name of the Nexudus ID field in your HubSpot, which fields sync between HubSpot and Nexudus, and which system "wins" for each one.

5. NetSuite: for the Finance Team

NetSuite holds the accounts. The Site P&L can only split income and costs by site if every line says which site it belongs to.

✅ Do❌ Don'tWhat goes wrong if you don't
Tag every line (bills, invoices, journals) with the site.Leave the site empty on "small" lines or on journals.The cost sits in "no site". Site profit looks better than it is.
Share central costs out to sites with the agreed method, every month.Change the method part way through the year without telling anyone.Months cannot be compared.
Post to the right period. Once a month is closed, put corrections in the next open month.Reopen a closed month to change it.A "final" number in the board pack changes after people have seen it.
Use the agreed income and cost accounts.Post meeting room income to the office income account "because it is close".Revenue lands on the wrong line of the P&L.

❓ Fill in for your company: which NetSuite field means "site" (it could be location, department, class or something else; companies set this up differently).

6. The Excel Budget and Capacity Sheet

The budget and the number of desks you can sell come from a spreadsheet. The data system reads it every night, so it expects the same shape every time.

✅ Do❌ Don't
Keep the template: same tab name, same columns, same order.Rename, move, add or delete columns, or merge cells.
Pick site names from the list.Type "Soho ", "soho" or "Soho site".
Have one owner for the sheet, written down.Let several people keep their own copies and send them round.
Put numbers only in number cells.Type "tbc" or "about 30" where a number should go.
Tell the data owner first if the layout needs to change.Change it and hope.

💡 If the sheet changes shape, the nightly check stops and the dashboards keep showing the last good numbers until it is fixed. That is safer than showing wrong numbers, but the budget figures will be out of date until someone puts the sheet back.

7. ACT

"ACT" can mean a door-access system or a CRM, and your company knows which one it uses. Ask the data owner if you are not sure. The rules below apply to door access.

✅ Do❌ Don't
Keep access in step with contracts: give access when a contract starts, remove it the day it ends.Leave a leaver's card working "just in case".
Put each card in the group for the right site and office.Use one "all doors" group for members.
Treat door data as personal data. It is only ever reported per company, for groups of 5 or more people.Ask for, or share, a list of when one person came in.

In Mini Work.Life, both Brightline cards are marked "removed" now that the contract has ended. That is exactly right.

Quick Check

1. A member moves from a hot desk to a private office at the same site. How do you enter it?
As an upgrade for the same company, not as a cancel plus a new customer. Otherwise it shows as a lost customer.

2. You cannot find a company in HubSpot by name. Should you create it?
Not yet. Search by email and website too; names are often written differently. Only create it if it is truly not there.

3. Finance spots an error in a month that is already closed. What now?
Put the correction in the next open month. Do not reopen the closed month.

4. Why must you tell the data owner before adding a column to the budget sheet?
The nightly load expects a fixed layout. A surprise change stops the budget figures from updating.
