Business Guide 04 — Fresh, Provisional or Final? Why Numbers Change

Hi again! 🙏 Have you ever seen a number on Monday and a different one for the same month on Friday? That is usually not a mistake. Numbers go through stages: fresh (from last night), provisional (the month is still open) and final (finance has closed the month). This guide explains each stage and what you can safely quote.

⚠️ About the examples. Numbers come from Mini Work.Life (made up), September 2026. Labels used here: ✅ confirmed (brief) means it is stated in Work.Life's scoping brief; 🛠 our standard means it is how the data team sets things up; ❓ ask your finance team means only your company can answer it.

1. The Nightly Refresh and "Last Updated"

Today, reports are built by hand and do not update themselves (✅ confirmed, brief: reporting is "static" and rebuilt by hand). With the data system, every night the data is copied from Nexudus, NetSuite, ACT, HubSpot and the Excel sheets into one place, checked, and the dashboards are rebuilt (🛠 our standard).

  • Anything entered today shows up tomorrow morning. A contract signed at 11am is not on the dashboard until the next day.
  • Every dashboard shows a "last updated" time. If it is not this morning, the nightly copy did not finish. Tell the data owner and wait before using the numbers.
  • If a nightly check fails (for example, revenue for a site suddenly drops to nothing), the data team is warned first. They may hold back the update rather than show you a wrong number (🛠 our standard).

Who decidesFinance. They do the month-end close: the checklist of steps that turns a month's records into official numbers.

A close often takes about 5 to 10 working days after the month ends for a company of Work.Life's size (general practice). ❓ Ask your finance team: how many working days your close takes, and on which day each month is locked.

3. Why Last Month's Number Can Still Change

Until the month is closed, finance is still adding things that belong to it. Here is what usually changes:

WhatIn plain wordsEffect
Late billsA supplier's bill for September arrives in October. It still belongs to September.September costs go up.
AccrualsThe bill has not arrived yet, so finance puts in a careful estimate so the cost lands in the right month.Costs go up now, and are adjusted when the real bill comes.
Credit notesA refund or discount given after an invoice, for example for a broken room.Revenue goes down.
Journals (finance adjustments)Entries finance makes at month end, such as site staff costs from payroll and each site's share of central costs.Site costs go up.
CorrectionsSomething was posted to the wrong site or account and is moved.One site goes up, another goes down.

Worked example. The final Soho numbers for September are from Mini Work.Life. The "2 October" view is made up to show the idea: suppose that on 2 October the credit note and the site staff journal had not been posted yet.

Nobody made a mistake. The first view was work in progress. If someone had quoted "Soho lost about £2,900" in a meeting on 2 October, they would have been about £1,300 out.

💡 Very rarely, finance finds a big error after a month is closed and restates it (reopens it, fixes it, locks it again). When that happens, finance should tell leadership the old and new figures (❓ ask your finance team how they share this).

4. The Provisional Label

When you pick an open month, the dashboard shows:

⚠ PROVISIONAL: September 2026 is not closed yet.
  Late bills, accruals, credit notes and finance journals can still change these numbers.
  Last closed month: August 2026.
  • When no month is picked, dashboards open on the last closed month (🛠 our standard), so the first thing you see is final.
  • The label comes from finance's own "closed" setting in the accounts, not from a guess about the date (🛠 our standard).
  • Space and member numbers (desks filled, members) come from Nexudus and do not wait for the finance close. They still only update each night.

5. MRR and Revenue: Different Timing

MRR is a snapshot: the monthly price of every contract active on the last day of the month. Revenue is what the accounts count as earned during the month. So a contract that starts part-way through a month shows up differently in each.

Example (made up): a new hot desk member at £320 a month starts on 20 September.

NumberSeptemberOctober
MRR+£320 (the contract is active on 30 September)+£320
RevenueAbout £117 if the first month is charged for 11 of 30 days (£320 × 11 ÷ 30)£320

Both numbers are right. MRR tells you where income is heading; revenue tells you what was earned. ❓ Ask your finance team whether first months are charged for part of the month, and how that shows in the accounts.

6. Why Nexudus Invoices and NetSuite Revenue Can Differ

Nexudus is where members are billed. NetSuite holds the accounts. The two totals for a month often differ a little, and that is normal when the difference can be explained.

ReasonExample
A credit note was raised in the accounts after the invoiceSoho: invoiced £3,565, revenue £3,475. The £90 difference is the goodwill credit note for the screen failure on 24 September (lab).
Timing: an invoice dated in one month covers anotherA quarter paid in advance is billed once but counted as revenue month by month.
Items that are not revenueA deposit is money held, not money earned.
A mistake in one systemAn invoice raised against the wrong site. This is the kind of difference finance looks for when it checks the two systems against each other at month end.

The dashboards show revenue from the accounts. If you see a gap you cannot explain, use the form in Guide 06 rather than fixing it in your own spreadsheet.

7. What to Quote in a Board Meeting

SituationSay this
The month is closed"September: Soho revenue £3,475, contribution −£4,175." No label needed.
The month is still open"October so far, provisional: ..." and say it can still change.
Mixing monthsUse only closed months for totals and trends. Do not add an open month to a quarter total without saying so.
Quoting a number from an exportSay the date of the export and whether the month was closed then.
A closed month was restatedGive the new number and say it was restated, with the old figure.

💡 Simple rule: board numbers come from closed months. If you must use an open month, say the word "provisional" out loud.

Quick Check

1. A contract was signed at 3pm today. When will it show on the dashboard?

Tomorrow morning, after the nightly refresh.

2. Who decides when a month is closed?

Finance, at the end of their month-end close.

3. Soho's September revenue dropped by £90 after you first looked. Why?

A £90 goodwill credit note was posted during the close.

4. A £320 contract starts on 20 September. Does September MRR go up by £320?

Yes, because it is active on 30 September. September revenue goes up by less if the first month is charged for part of the month.

5. The board meets on 3 October. Can you present September as final?

Only if finance has closed it. If not, call it provisional, or use August, the last closed month.
