Business Guide 07 — Your Routine, Role by Role

Hi again! 🙏 The dashboards help most when looking at them becomes a habit, a few minutes at the right moments. This last guide gives each role a simple routine, shows how the month-end works, and gives you a ready-made 30-minute monthly numbers meeting.

⚠️ About the examples. All example numbers come from Mini Work.Life, the small made-up company with two sites (Soho and Reading). The routines and working-day timings are our suggested starting point (made up). Change them to fit your company.

What we will cover in this guide:

  • Routines for leadership, finance, site and operations managers, sales, and the data owner
  • The month-end rhythm
  • How to run a 30-minute monthly numbers meeting
  • Getting a new starter going
  • A one-page cheat sheet

7. How to Run a 30-Minute Monthly Numbers Meeting

Open the dashboards on a shared screen and go through them in this order. Everyone sees the same numbers, so the time goes on decisions, not on arguing about whose spreadsheet is right.

TimeItemDashboardExample talking points (Mini Work.Life, September 2026)
0–3 minIs the month final?Leadership Overview (last updated time)"September is closed. These are final numbers."
3–12 minHow did each site do?Leadership Overview, then Site P&L"Soho made a contribution of −£4,175 (−£5,175 after central costs) and is £5,725 (62%) under budget. Desk occupancy is only 17.6%, 6 of 34 desks. Reading made +£450 before central costs."
12–18 minMembers in and outMembers & Retention"Only one company left this year, Brightline, £1,400 a month, reason 'moving to hybrid'. Pixel Chai's move was an upgrade and Kite & Key's was a transfer, so neither is a loss."
18–25 minPipeline and renewalsSales Pipeline, Attendance (only if agreed)"Open pipeline is £7,000 a month: the GreenRoot renewal at £2,800 and Ortho Robotics at £4,200. GreenRoot's contract ends 31 December." If Attendance is agreed: "GreenRoot's weekly badge-in rate fell 73% → 40% → 23% → about 0. That is a company-level warning sign. Let's call them this week."
25–28 minMeeting roomsMeeting Rooms"Soho's Brick Lane room was booked 9 of 220 open hours (4.1%). Promote it, or use the space another way?"
28–30 minActions(none)Up to three actions, each with an owner and a date. Any number that looked wrong goes to the data owner (Guide 06).

⚠️ About attendance: door data is personal data. Only talk about it if your company has agreed the Attendance dashboard, and only per company for groups of 5 or more people. Never per person. GreenRoot has 6 people, so it can be shown.

💡 Tips: send the dashboard links the day before; do not paste numbers into slides (they go out of date and drift from the dashboards); write the actions down where everyone can see them.

Quick Check

1. Finance has not closed the month yet. Should leadership hold the monthly numbers meeting?
Better to wait. Before the close, numbers are provisional and can still change.

2. Which dashboard do you open to talk about a site losing money?
Leadership Overview first for the summary, then Site P&L for the line-by-line detail.

3. Can you talk about one member's door visits in the meeting?
No. Door data is personal. Only company-level totals for groups of 5 or more, and only if Attendance has been agreed.

4. What should sales do every week to keep the pipeline right?
Update each open deal's stage, amount and close date, close old deals with a reason, and merge duplicates.
