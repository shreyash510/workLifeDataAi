Business Guide 06 — When a Number Looks Wrong, and Asking for Something New

Hi again! 🙏 Sooner or later you will look at a dashboard and think "that cannot be right". Good! Spotting odd numbers is how the data stays healthy. This guide shows a quick self-check, how to report a problem in two minutes, what happens after you report it, and how to ask for a new chart or a new number.

⚠️ About the examples. All example numbers come from Mini Work.Life, the small made-up company with two sites (Soho and Reading). Response times in this guide are made-up targets; your company will agree its own.

What we will cover in this guide:

  • A 5-step self-check before you report
  • How to report a problem (copy-paste template)
  • What happens after you report it
  • The usual causes, in plain words
  • Asking for a new chart, report or number
  • What not to do

1. A 5-Step Self-Check Before You Report

Most "wrong" numbers are right numbers answering a different question. Spend one minute on these five checks first.

#CheckMini Work.Life example
1Right filter? Which site, which product, which team is selected?Soho desk occupancy is 17.6% (6 of 34). Both sites together are 18.5% (12 of 65). If a filter is still set to Soho, the "company" number looks low.
2Right month? Is the dashboard on the month you think?Many dashboards open on the last closed month, not this month.
3Provisional? Is the month still open?An open month can still change until finance closes it (Guide 04).
4Last updated time? Look at the time on the dashboard.A contract signed this morning shows up tomorrow. If the time is more than a day old, the nightly load may have stopped. That is worth reporting.
5Like with like? Are you comparing the same kind of number?Soho in September: MRR £3,040, invoiced £3,565, revenue in the accounts £3,475. Three different numbers, all correct (Guide 03).

💡 Another common one: "how many members does Soho have?" Counted by the site of their contract, Soho has 6. Counted by the company's home site, Soho has 5. Check which one the dashboard says it uses.

If all five checks pass and the number still looks wrong, report it. You are not wasting anyone's time.

2. How to Report a Problem

Copy this, fill it in, and send it to the data owner. A good report gets fixed much faster than "the numbers are off".

SUBJECT: Number looks wrong – [dashboard name]

Dashboard:            Site P&L
Filters I used:       site = Soho, month = September 2026
Number I see:         contribution –£4,175
Number I expected:    about –£3,000
Where my number
comes from:           my own note of the September costs / last
                      month's board pack / the Nexudus report ...
Last updated time
on the dashboard:     8 Oct 2026, 06:10
Screenshot:           attached (no member names, emails or
                      personal details showing)
How urgent:           needed for the board pack on [date] / not urgent

The "expected" number and the date in this example are made up, just to show how to fill it in.

⚠️ Screenshots: crop or cover any names, emails or other personal details before you send them. Company names and totals are fine.

❓ Fill in for your company: where reports go (an email address, a form or a chat channel).

5. Asking for a New Chart, Report or Number

New requests are welcome. Start with the decision, not the chart. Copy this:

SUBJECT: Request – [short name]

The decision I want to make:   which renewals to call first each month
The question I need answered:  which contracts end in the next 90 days,
                               and how much income is at stake?
How often I need it:           monthly, first week
Who should see it:             sales and site managers
Does it use personal data?     no / yes (say which and why)
Is there a number like this
already?                       I checked Guide 03: not that I can see

A new number needs one agreed meaning first. Suppose you ask for "active members by site". Mini Work.Life already shows the problem: by contract site it is Soho 6, Reading 6; by home site it is Soho 5, Reading 7. Both are fair answers. Before anything is built, the data owner will ask:

  • Exactly how is it counted? (What is in, what is left out?)
  • Which system does it come from?
  • Who owns the meaning? (Finance for money, Operations for members and occupancy.)

Once agreed, the meaning is written in Guide 03 and used everywhere. That is how everyone keeps seeing the same number.

🛠 A small change to an existing chart might take days; a brand-new number with a new meaning might take a few weeks (made-up targets).

6. What Not to Do

❌ Please don'tWhy✅ Do this instead
Keep your own private spreadsheet copy with "fixed" numbersNow there are two truths, and only you know why yours differs.Report the problem so it is fixed for everyone.
Export numbers, change them, and send them roundPeople think they are the official numbers.Share a link to the dashboard, or say clearly that your numbers are your own estimate.
Ask for personal data you do not need (names, door times of one person)It is personal data. Company-level totals are almost always enough.Ask for the totals per company or site.
Quote an open month as finalIt can still change.Say "provisional" until finance closes it.
Ask the data team to "just change the number on the dashboard"It comes back wrong the next night.Fix the entry in the source system.

Quick Check

1. Soho MRR is £3,040 but the Site P&L shows revenue of £3,475. Is something broken?
No. MRR is what contracts promise; revenue is what finance counts in the accounts. Different questions, both right.

2. Name three things to put in a problem report.
Any three of: the dashboard, the filters, the number you see, the number you expected, where your number comes from, the last updated time, a screenshot with no personal details.

3. Where does the fix go?
Into the system where the data was entered, never into the dashboard or a spreadsheet.

4. Why can't a new number be built straight away?
It first needs one agreed meaning, a source and an owner, or two teams will count it two ways.
