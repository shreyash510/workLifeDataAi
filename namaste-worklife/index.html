🏢 Namaste Work.Life: Zero to Hero — A Real Data & AI Project, End to End

This series takes one real client project and explains every piece of it. Work.Life is a UK coworking company. They want their leadership team to ask questions about the business in plain English and trust the answers. To build that, you need to understand the business, every system its data lives in (Nexudus, NetSuite, ACT, Excel, HubSpot), how that data travels (Fivetran, Python, BigQuery, dbt), how it is shown (Tableau, Metabase), and then the AI layer on top.

Simple language, one topic per chapter, and in Season 1 you run a Mini Work.Life yourself in every tool. By the end you could sit in the client call and answer every question.
